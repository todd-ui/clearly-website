Important Disclaimer

These calculations are estimates only based on New York's statutory guideline formulas (DRL §236 and §240). Actual court-ordered amounts may differ based on judicial discretion, deviation factors, above-cap income considerations, add-on expenses (childcare, medical, education), and the specific circumstances of your case.
