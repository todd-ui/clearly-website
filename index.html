[01] The problem

Separation doesn't reduce communication. It increases it.

Pickup times, doctors, school forms, expenses, holidays. You'll talk to this person more than you expect, for longer than you expect. And most of the time you agree on more than you think. It's the way the conversation goes that keeps burying it.

Every text about Tuesday pickup carries the weight of everything that came before it.

A typical Tuesday

  1. 9:02SamCan you do pickup at 3 instead of 5 on Friday?
  2. 9:47AlexWhy does it always have to change?
  3. 10:15SamYou said you'd be flexible. You never are.
  4. 10:16AlexThis is exactly what you always do.

One question. Four messages. No answer.

[02] How Clearly is different

Other apps keep a record of the fight. Clearly helps you end it.

Most co-parenting apps are a shared inbox with a paper trail. In Clearly, every conversation is a topic with one job: get you both to an answer.

  1. 1

    Raise it.

    Start a topic: a question, an FYI, or something you need to decide together.

    "Can Maya stay Saturday night for her cousin's birthday?"
  2. 2

    Say it calmly.

    Before it's posted, Clearly checks the tone and suggests calmer wording. You choose what to send.

    "You ALWAYS do this."
    "Can we confirm the time?"
  3. 3

    Answer it.

    A question gets a one-tap Yes or No. A bigger topic gets a proposed resolution, in plain words.

    "Yes." · one tap
  4. 4

    It's settled.

    You both accept the same written answer. It's timestamped and stays on the record as written.

    ✓ Resolved · both accepted

Built on the principles of mediation and the Harvard Negotiation Project's Getting to Yes.

[05] Questions

Before you download.

More in the full FAQ →

Does my co-parent have to pay?

No. One subscription covers both parents. Your co-parent creates their own account and joins free with your invite code.

Does Clearly take sides?

Never. Clearly checks the tone of your own message before you post and suggests calmer wording. It never labels a parent, and you always choose what to send.

Can I export the record?

Yes. Every topic and reply is timestamped and kept exactly as written. You can export a PDF report for the last 30 days, 90 days or all time, or all your data as a spreadsheet.

Is Clearly on Android?

Not yet. Clearly is on iPhone. Your schedule can be added to Apple or Google Calendar with a private calendar link.

What happens if we stop subscribing?

Clearly becomes read-only. Your records stay viewable and exportable. You can try the free demo first, no account needed.
