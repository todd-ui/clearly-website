[01] The problem

Separation doesn't reduce communication. It increases it.

Pickups. Dentists. Permission slips. Who has Thanksgiving. You'll talk to your co-parent more than you ever expected. Most of it isn't the hard part. How it goes is.

Every text about Tuesday pickup carries the weight of everything that came before it.

Can you do pickup at 3 instead? Dentist moved to Tuesday Soccer fees due Friday · $140 Who has Thanksgiving this year? Can we swap weekends? Did she take her medicine? Her coat is still at your place Who's paying for the tutor? You said you'd be here at 5. Can you send the receipt? We agreed on this already. This is exactly what you always do.

[02] How Clearly is different

Other apps keep a record of the fight. Clearly helps you end it.

In Clearly, every conversation is a topic. And every topic has one job: get you both to an answer.

  1. 1

    Raise it.

    Ask a question, share an update, or put something on the table.

    "Can Maya stay Saturday night for her cousin's birthday?"
  2. 2

    Say it calmly.

    Clearly reads it before your co-parent does and offers a calmer way to say it. Your words, your call.

    "You ALWAYS do this."
    "Can we confirm the time?"
  3. 3

    Answer it.

    Yes or No takes one tap. Bigger things get a proposed resolution, in plain words.

    "Yes." · one tap
  4. 4

    Settle it.

    You both accept the same words. Timestamped. Kept exactly as written. Done.

    ✓ Resolved · both accepted

Built on the principles of mediation and the Harvard Negotiation Project's Getting to Yes.

[05] Questions

Good questions.

The ones parents ask before they download. More in the full FAQ →

Does my co-parent have to pay?

Nope. One subscription covers you both. Your co-parent joins free with your invite code.

Does Clearly take sides?

Never. Clearly doesn't label anyone or pick a winner. The tone check suggests calmer wording for your own message, and you always decide what to send.

Can I export the record?

Yes. Every topic and reply is timestamped and kept exactly as written. Export a PDF report for the last 30 days, 90 days or all time, or everything as a spreadsheet.

Is Clearly on Android?

Not yet. Clearly is on iPhone. You can still add your schedule to Apple or Google Calendar with a private link.

What happens if we stop subscribing?

Clearly goes read-only. Your records stay viewable and exportable. Want to look around first? Try the free demo, no account needed.
