[01] The problem

Separation doesn't reduce communication. It increases it.

Pickups. Dentists. Permission slips. Who has Thanksgiving. You'll talk to your co-parent more than you ever expected. Most of it isn't the hard part. How it goes is.

Every text about Tuesday pickup carries the weight of everything that came before it.

Can you do pickup at 3 instead? Dentist moved to Tuesday Soccer fees due Friday · $140 Who has Thanksgiving this year? Can we swap weekends? Did she take her medicine? Her coat is still at your place Who's paying for the tutor? You said you'd be here at 5. Can you send the receipt? We agreed on this already. This is exactly what you always do.

[02] How Clearly is different

Other apps give you a thread. Clearly gets you to an answer.

Every conversation in Clearly is a topic with one job: get to an answer you both accept. Most go straight through. When one starts to go sideways, Clearly steps in like a mediator would, and only then. So decisions get made, and the kids stay out of the middle.

    Raise it.

    One topic, one question.

    What they type“You NEVER tell me anything about Ava’s school!”
  2. 2

    Only when it’s needed.

    Reframed by Clearly“Can you add me to Ava’s school emails?”
  3. 3

Stuck? Clearly shows where it stands and suggests options based on what you’ve both said.

[07] Questions

Good questions.

The ones parents ask before they download. More in the full FAQ →

Does my co-parent have to pay?

Nope. One subscription covers you both. Your co-parent joins free with your invite code.

Does Clearly take sides?

Never. Clearly doesn't label anyone or pick a winner. When Clearly helps with wording, it's for your own message, and you always decide what to send.

Does Clearly work in my language?

Yes. Write in Spanish, Chinese, Tagalog or most other languages, and Clearly’s suggested wording and written-up agreements come back in your language. The app’s menus are in English for now.

Can I export the record?

Yes. Every topic and reply is timestamped and kept exactly as written. Export the whole record, or an exhibit of just what matters, as a court-ready PDF, or everything as a spreadsheet. Every page carries a document ID that anyone can check at getclearly.app/verify.

Is Clearly on Android?

Yes, in your browser. Clearly on the web works on any Android phone or computer at web.getclearly.app, with the same account and record as the iPhone app. Add it to your home screen in Chrome and it opens like an app.

What happens if we stop subscribing?

Clearly goes read-only. Your records stay viewable and exportable. Want to look around first? Try the free demo, no account needed.
