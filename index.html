What nobody tells you

Separation doesn't reduce communication. It increases it.

Pickup times, doctors, school forms, expenses, holidays. You'll talk to this person more than you expect, about more than you expect, for longer than you expect.

And most of the time you agree on more than you think. You both love your kids. It's the way the conversation goes that keeps burying it.

Every text about Tuesday pickup carries the weight of everything that came before it.

How Clearly is different

Other apps keep a record of the fight. Clearly helps you end it.

Most co-parenting apps are a shared inbox with a paper trail. Clearly changes how the conversation goes.

01

A second look before you post.

Clearly checks the tone of your message and suggests a calmer way to say it. Use it or keep your own words. You choose what to send.

What you wrote

"You're ALWAYS late. Every single time. I have a life too and you have zero respect for my time or for these kids."

✦ Clearly suggests

A calmer way to say it

"Drop-off has been late on several recent occasions. Please confirm you can make the agreed time going forward."

02

Every topic ends in an answer.

Questions get a one-tap Yes or No. Bigger conversations end in a written resolution you both accept, so nobody has to ask "what did we decide?" again.

Can Maya stay with me Saturday night for her cousin's birthday? I'll drop her at yours by 10am Sunday.

The record stays exactly as written.

Topics and replies can't be edited or deleted. Every one is timestamped, and you can export a PDF report whenever you need one.
