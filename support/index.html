Popular articles

  • What Clearly is and how the pieces fitClearly is a co-parenting app for two parents, on the web and iPhone, with a free read-only portal for the professionals they choose.
  • Use Clearly on the webSign in at web.getclearly.app in any current browser, on a computer, Android phone, iPad or tablet.
  • How topics workEvery conversation is a topic about one thing, and it ends in an answer or a resolution you both accept.
  • Set up or change your custody schedulePick a pattern such as week on, week off or 2-2-3, say who starts, and set the exchange time.
  • Log an expenseAdd what it was for, the amount and a category. Clearly splits it using your agreed split, and your co-parent is told.
  • Export your record as a PDF or spreadsheetDownload your full record, a single topic or a spreadsheet, for the last 30 days, 90 days or all time. Exports are always free.
  • Invite your lawyer, mediator or therapistGive a professional free, read-only access to the areas you choose. Your co-parent is told.
  • Create a free professional accountFamily lawyers, mediators, therapists and parenting coordinators sign up free at web.getclearly.app/pro.
  • Plans and prices$24.99 a month or $249 a year in the US. One subscription covers both parents, on the web and iPhone.
  • I can't sign inCheck the email you used, reset your password, confirm your email, and make sure you're on the right sign-in page.
  • Create your Clearly accountSign up on the web or in the iPhone app with your name, email and a password.
  • Start a topicWrite what you need in plain words. Clearly gives it a short title and the right reply buttons.
  • Accept a parent's invitationOpen the link in the invitation email, sign in or create your account with the invited email, and accept.
  • The free trialNew subscribers get 14 days free. A card is needed to start, and you aren't charged if you cancel before it ends.
  • Reset a forgotten passwordUse Forgot password on the sign-in screen and follow the link in the email.
  • Set up your familyAdd your kids, set the custody schedule, holidays and summer, and agree the expense split. It takes about five minutes.
  • Invite a family to ClearlyEmail a parent an invitation from you, with the areas you'd like them to share and an optional free month.
  • One subscription covers both parentsWhichever parent pays covers the other, on the web and iPhone. You can't be charged twice for the same family.
  • Invite your co-parentSend an invite by email, by text or any app, or share your family code. Your subscription covers them.
  • When Clearly holds a message before it's sentWhen a message is likely to make things harder, Clearly doesn't post it as written and suggests other wording. You choose what to send.
  • Request or answer a swapAsk to change days with your co-parent. They accept or decline, and an accepted swap updates the calendar for both of you.
  • Delete your accountDelete your account from Settings. If your co-parent stays, the shared record stays in their account without your name.
  • Join your co-parent's familyUse the link or 6-character code your co-parent sent. Joining is free, and you get their setup straight away.
  • Reach a resolution togetherWhen a topic is ready to end, one of you proposes how, and the other agrees or says not yet.
  • Cancel your subscriptionCancel on the web from Settings, or through Apple if you subscribed on iPhone. You keep access to the end of the paid period.
  • Can messages be edited or deleted?No. Topics, replies and answers stay exactly as written, with times recorded by the server.
