See whether your co-parent has read a topic

Clearly shows when a topic was delivered and when your co-parent opened it.

iPhone appWeb app

On iPhone: one check mark means sent. Two check marks mean your co-parent has opened the topic.

On the web: messages show Read with the time, or Delivered · not read yet. In a topic's side panel, Read by shows when each of you last opened it.

Read receipts are always on, for both of you.
