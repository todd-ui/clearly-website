What Clearly is

Clearly is a co-parenting app for iPhone and the web. It helps co-parents share a custody schedule, raise and resolve topics, track shared expenses, and keep their parenting plan, kids' details and contacts in one place. A parent can also give a family professional, such as a lawyer, mediator or therapist, a free, read-only view of the family's record.
