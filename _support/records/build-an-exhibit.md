An exhibit is a timeline of just the entries you need, such as everything about school pickups since March.

## On the web

1. Open **Records** and find `Build an exhibit`.
2. Set `From` and `To`, choose `Both parents` or one parent, and add a keyword if you like.
3. Choose what to include: messages, outcomes, schedule changes and expenses.
4. Tick `Only flagged entries` to see only entries with a flag, such as no reply for 2 or more days, a resolution not accepted, a declined swap or a disputed expense.
5. Click `Build timeline`, check it, then click `Exhibit PDF` or `CSV`.

## On iPhone

1. Open **Settings**, then `Export data`, then `Build an exhibit (PDF)`.
2. Set the dates, who it was written by, and words it contains.
3. Choose what to include, and whether to show flagged entries only.
4. Tap `Create exhibit (PDF)`.

Flags are facts from the record. Clearly doesn't judge who was right.
