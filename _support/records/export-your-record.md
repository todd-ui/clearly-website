Exporting is always free, with or without a subscription.

## On the web

1. In the sidebar, open **Records**.
2. Choose `Last 30 days`, `Last 90 days` or `All time`.
3. Under `For every PDF`, choose `Children's full names` or `Initials only`, and optionally a state for the declaration.
4. Choose what to download:
   - **Full record (PDF):** click `Create PDF`.
   - **Spreadsheets:** `Topics`, `Expenses` or `Schedule & swaps` as CSV files.

## On iPhone

1. Open **Settings**, then **Support**, then `Export data`.
2. Choose the record range, and how children are named in the PDF.
3. Tap `Full record (PDF)`, `Build an exhibit (PDF)` or `All data (CSV)`.
4. The file opens in the share sheet, so you can save it to Files, print it, or send it.

## What's in a PDF

Every page carries a document ID, so anyone can check the PDF is genuine and unchanged. See [Check a Clearly document](/support/verify-a-document).

> Note: Clearly keeps your record as it happened. Whether and how a record is used in court is a question for your lawyer.
