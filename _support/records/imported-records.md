On the web, **Records** has a `From other apps` section. Click `Add a record` to upload a PDF, image, CSV, text or Word file up to 25 MB, with its name, where it's from and the dates it covers.

- The file is kept exactly as you uploaded it.
- It's labeled `Imported` with its source, so it's never mistaken for something written in Clearly.
- It isn't turned into Clearly messages.
- Only the parent who uploaded it can remove it. Removing deletes it.

Adding a record needs a subscription. Imported records are included when you share the **Records** area with a professional.
