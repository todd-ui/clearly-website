On **Professionals**, `What they've viewed` lists what each professional opened, for example:

- A section, such as the schedule or expenses
- A topic, by its subject
- A search, with the words they searched
- An export

Repeat views within a few minutes show as one entry. Both parents see the same list. `Past access` shows professionals whose access has ended.
