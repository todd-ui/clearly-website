You choose each area a professional can see:

- **Topics:** topics, replies, read receipts and resolutions.
- **Messages paused for wording:** a count over time of messages Clearly held back. Never the words, which aren't kept.
- **Schedule:** the parenting schedule, events and swap requests.
- **Expenses:** shared expenses, receipts and payments.
- **Family info:** children, contacts and places.
- **Records:** PDFs of the record, plus downloads of topics, expenses, the calendar and imported documents.

## Always private

A professional sees parents by name only. They never see your email address, payment details, subscription or family code.

## They can't take part

Professionals can't post, reply, answer, log or change anything. Nothing they do shows in your conversations.
