A professional can look, but never post, change or delete anything. Access is free for them.

## Invite them

**On the web:** in the sidebar, open **Professionals** and click `Invite a professional`. **On iPhone:** open **Settings**, then **Professionals**, then `Invite a professional`.

1. Enter `Their email`.
2. Choose their role: family lawyer, mediator, therapist, parenting coordinator, guardian ad litem, custody evaluator, social worker, coach or other professional.
3. Under `What they can see`, check the areas. Clearly suggests areas for each role, and you can change them.
4. Optionally set when `Access ends`. Leave it open to keep access until you end it.
5. Add a note to them if you like, and send it.

They get an email to create a free professional account, or sign in, and accept.

## Your co-parent is told

When you invite a professional, your co-parent is told who and in what role. Both of you can see who has access and what they've viewed.

> Note: A professional uses a separate professional account. They can't use an email that belongs to a parent in Clearly.
