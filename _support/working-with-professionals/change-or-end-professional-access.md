Open **Professionals** (the sidebar on the web, **Settings** on iPhone) and find the professional under `Who has access`.

## Change what they see

Use `Change access` (or `Change what they can see` on iPhone). Only the parent who invited them can do this. Your co-parent is told.

## End access

Use `End access`, or `Cancel invite` for one they haven't accepted yet.

**Either parent** can end access for these roles, because they work with both of you:

- Mediator
- Parenting coordinator
- Guardian ad litem
- Custody evaluator

**Only the parent who invited them** can end access for these, because they're one parent's own:

- Family lawyer
- Therapist
- Coach
- Social worker
- Other professional

## After access ends

They don't see anything new from that moment. They keep a read-only copy of your record as it was then, like a case file. Both parents are told.

Access also ends on its own on the end date, if you set one.
