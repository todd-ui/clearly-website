If your lawyer, mediator or another professional invited you, their email has a `Get started` button.

1. Tap `Get started`. If you already use Clearly, it signs you in.
2. Set up your family, or join your co-parent's.
3. Clearly asks whether to share with that professional. Tick the areas you're comfortable with, then:
   - `Share read-only access` to share now.
   - `Not now` to decide later.
   - `No thanks` to decline. They're told.

Nothing is shared unless you say yes. If you share, your co-parent is told.

## A free month

If the professional included a free month, it starts when your family is set up, whether or not you share. See [A free month from a professional](/support/free-month-from-a-professional).

Clearly never pays professionals for inviting families.
