Inviting your co-parent needs a subscription, and that subscription covers them too. They never pay to join.

## On the web

1. On **Today**, find `Invite your co-parent`, or go to **Settings** and then **Co-parent**.
2. To have Clearly send it, enter `Their email` and click `Send invite`.
3. To send it yourself, use `Copy invite`, `Text it` (on a phone) or `Share…`, or copy your `Family code`.

Clearly reminds them after 3 days, and again the day before the invite expires. An emailed invite lasts 30 days.

While an invite is waiting you can `Resend`, `Change email` or `Cancel invite`. Only one emailed invite can be waiting at a time. Your family code keeps working even if you cancel an emailed invite.

## In the iPhone app

1. On **Today**, tap `Invite`, or open **Settings** (your initial at the top right) and go to **Co-parent**.
2. Choose `Text`, `Email` or `Another app`, or tap `Invite code` to copy it.

## What they get

The invite contains a link like getclearly.app/join/ABC234 and your 6-character family code. Tapping the link on their phone opens Clearly with the code filled in. They can also enter the code by hand. See [Join your co-parent's family](/support/join-your-co-parents-family).

When they join, you're told, and **Settings** shows you as connected.

> Note: A family has two parents at most. If your co-parent left Clearly, you can invite someone again.
