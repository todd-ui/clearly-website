The parent who starts on Clearly sets up the family. The co-parent who joins later uses the same setup and can change it afterwards.

## On the web

After you create your account, Clearly asks whether your co-parent has already started.

1. Choose `No, I'm starting`.
2. Name your family if you like. Leave it empty and Clearly uses both parents' names.
3. Start your free trial now, or choose `Skip for now` and set things up first.
4. On **Today**, the `Set up your family` card lists the steps:
   - `Add your kids`
   - `Set the custody schedule`
   - `Choose your holidays` (or `Not needed`)
   - `Set the summer schedule` (or `Not needed`)
   - `Agree the expense split`
   - Invite your co-parent

Each step opens the right page under **Shared** in the sidebar.

## In the iPhone app

1. On `Getting started`, choose `Start fresh`.
2. **Your children:** add each child's first name and birthdate. A family name is optional.
3. **Custody schedule:** pick a pattern, say who starts, check the preview and set the exchange time.
4. **Summer:** choose `No, same all year` or `Yes, summer is different`.
5. **Holiday schedule:** pick the holidays you alternate.
6. **Shared expenses:** set how you split costs for the kids.
7. **All set:** check the summary, then continue to start your trial and invite your co-parent.

## Change it later

Everything you set up can be changed. On the web, use **Shared** in the sidebar. On iPhone, use the **Shared** tab. Either parent can make changes.

> Note: Setting up is free. Inviting your co-parent, posting and logging need a subscription. One covers you both. See [What works without a subscription](/support/what-works-without-a-subscription).
