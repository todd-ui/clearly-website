If your co-parent already uses Clearly, ask them for an invite link or code first and follow [Join your co-parent's family](/support/join-your-co-parents-family) instead. Joining is free.

## On the web

1. Go to [web.getclearly.app/signup](https://web.getclearly.app/signup).
2. Enter your `First name`, `Last name`, `Email` and `Password`.
3. Tick `I agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy`.
4. Click `Create account`.

## In the iPhone app

1. Open Clearly and tap `Get started`.
2. Enter your `First name`, `Last name`, `Email` and `Create a password`.
3. Tap `Create account`.

## Password rules

Your password needs at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number. Clearly also turns down passwords that have appeared in a known data breach, because they are easy to guess. Only a short, scrambled piece of the password is checked; the password itself is never sent anywhere.

## Confirming your email

If you're asked to confirm your email, open the message from Clearly and tap the link, then sign in. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.

> Note: There is no Sign in with Apple or Google. You sign in with your email and password, on the web and iPhone alike.

> Tip: Use the same email on the web and in the iPhone app. It's one account, so everything you do in one shows up in the other.
