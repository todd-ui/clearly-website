The demo lets you try Clearly with a simulated co-parent before you create an account.

## Start the demo

1. Open the Clearly app.
2. Tap `Try it with a virtual co‑parent`.
3. Read how Clearly uses AI and tap `Agree and continue`.
4. A short tour starts. You are "Alex", and "Jordan" is the simulated co-parent, who replies a few seconds after you do. Demo replies are marked `Demo · simulated co-parent`.

## What you can't do in the demo

- Export records
- Add photos to replies
- Invite professionals
- Join a real family or subscribe

## Leave the demo

- Tap `Start free` to create your real account.
- Tap `Leave demo` to clear the demo. Anything you added in the demo is removed.

The demo lasts 14 days on your phone. When it ends you can restart it or exit.

> Note: The demo is in the iPhone app only. On the web, you start with a real account.
