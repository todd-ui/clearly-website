Joining is free. Your co-parent's subscription covers you both.

## With the link

1. Tap the link in the invite on your phone, or open it on a computer.
2. Create your account. The code is remembered, so you don't have to type it.
3. Clearly connects you to the family when you finish.

## With the code

**On the web:** create your account at [web.getclearly.app/signup](https://web.getclearly.app/signup), choose `Yes, or I have a code`, enter the code and click `Continue`.

**On iPhone:** on the first screen tap `I have an invite code`, enter the code, tap `Continue`, then create your account.

Family codes are 6 characters and never use the letters I or O or the numbers 0 or 1, so there's nothing to mix up.

## If you already set up on your own

**On iPhone:** open **Settings**, go to **Co-parent** and tap `Join with their code`, or tap your co-parent's link. Clearly asks you to confirm, because the schedule and children you set up on your own will no longer be shown. You'll use the family your co-parent set up.

## What you see after joining

You get the kids, schedule, holidays, summer and expense split your co-parent set up. Either of you can change them under **Shared**.

If the code doesn't work, see [My invite code doesn't work](/support/invite-code-not-working).
