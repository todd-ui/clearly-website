If you made a plan with the free [custody plan builder](https://getclearly.app/plan-builder/), it gave you a code. That code sets up your family in Clearly with what you chose.

1. Create your account at [web.getclearly.app/signup](https://web.getclearly.app/signup).
2. When Clearly asks whether your co-parent has already started, choose `Yes, or I have a code`.
3. Enter your plan code and click `Continue`.

Clearly creates your family with the schedule, holidays, summer, expense split, kids and payment method from your plan. You can change any of it under **Shared**.

If the code doesn't match, Clearly says so. Check it and try again. After several wrong codes you have to wait an hour.
