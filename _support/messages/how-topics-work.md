In Clearly you don't send loose messages. You start a topic about one thing, such as a pickup time, a school form or a doctor's visit, and it stays together until it's settled.

## Kinds of topic

When you write a topic, Clearly works out how your co-parent can answer it, based on what you wrote:

- **A quick question** they can answer Yes or No in one tap.
- **A question** they answer in a few words.
- **An FYI** they mark as seen.
- **A conversation**, for anything that needs back and forth.

To send it another way, change the wording.

## Statuses

Each topic shows where it is, for example:

- `Waiting for you` or `Waiting for co-parent`
- `Resolution pending`: one of you proposed how it ends.
- `Resolved`
- `Closed · not accepted`, `Closed · no reply` or `Closed · no activity`
- `No longer needed`

## Open and closed

**On iPhone,** Topics has `Open` and `Closed` tabs. **On the web,** filter by `Open`, `For you`, `Closed` or `All`.

A closed topic stays on the record. To raise it again, start a new topic.
