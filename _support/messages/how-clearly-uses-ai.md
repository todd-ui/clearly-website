Clearly uses AI from Anthropic for a few jobs:

- Working out what kind of topic you're starting and giving it a title.
- Checking each message before your co-parent sees it, and holding back anything hostile.
- Suggesting wording, drafting a reply and suggesting a resolution.
- Summing up where a conversation stands.

Everything it suggests is based on what you and your co-parent wrote. It doesn't decide anything for you. You choose what's posted, and you can always write your own words instead.

## Your information

What you write in topics, replies and answers is sent to Anthropic to provide these features. Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. The AI never sees your photos or receipts. See the [privacy policy](https://getclearly.app/privacy) for details.

The iPhone app asks you to agree to this once, when you first sign in.
