A conversation ends with a resolution you both accept. It goes on the record as what you agreed.

## On iPhone

1. When a conversation is ready, Clearly may offer a `Suggested resolution`, written from what you've both said. Tap `Propose` to send it, or `Edit` to write your own version first.
2. Your co-parent sees `Your co-parent proposed this`, with `Resolve` and `Not yet`.
3. While you wait, you see `Waiting on your co-parent`. Tap `Undo` to take your proposal back. Your co-parent is told.

If your co-parent taps `Not yet`, the topic goes back to talking and you're told it's not settled yet. A new reply from either of you also sets a proposal aside.

## On the web

1. In the topic's side panel, under `Wrap it up`, click `Propose a resolution`, write how it ends and click `Propose`.
2. Your co-parent sees `Agree and resolve` and `Propose something different`.

## Where this stands

On iPhone, `Where this stands` at the top of a conversation sums up what's settled and where each of you is, based on the conversation. Tap `Ask for an update` to refresh it.

## After it's resolved

If the resolution includes a date or a cost, the iPhone app can offer `Add to calendar` or `Add as expense`.

A proposal that isn't accepted within 30 days closes as `Closed · not accepted`. See [When topics close on their own](/support/when-topics-close-on-their-own).
