## On iPhone

You can close a topic you started until your co-parent replies.

1. Open the topic and tap **•••** at the top right, then `Close topic`.
2. Choose `Sorted` or `No longer needed`.

## On the web

1. Open the topic you started.
2. In the side panel, click `Withdraw topic` and confirm.

Either way, the topic stays on the record and your co-parent is told. Once your co-parent has replied, a topic ends with a resolution you both accept, or closes on its own after 30 quiet days.
