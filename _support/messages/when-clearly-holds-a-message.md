Clearly checks each message before your co-parent sees it. When a message is likely to make things harder between you, Clearly doesn't post it as written, and your co-parent doesn't see it. It says why, and suggests wording that keeps things moving.

## What you see

`Not posted as written` (or `Not sent as written` for a reply), with the reason and a suggestion.

- Tap or click `Use this wording` to send the suggestion.
- Choose `Edit my message` to change it yourself and try again.

Messages that were held aren't kept, and your co-parent never sees them. If you share the **Messages paused for wording** area with a professional, they see only a count over time, never the words.

## Get wording help before you send

**On iPhone,** type your reply, tap `Rewrite` and choose `Brief`, `Balanced` or `Warm`. Set your usual style in **Settings**, then `Reframing style`.

The suggestions are based on what you wrote and the conversation so far. You always decide what's sent.
