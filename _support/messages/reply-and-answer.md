## Quick answers

- A quick question shows `Yes` and `No`.
- A question that needs a few words shows a box for your answer.
- An FYI shows `OK` on iPhone or `OK, seen` on the web.

Once either of you writes a reply, the topic becomes a conversation and the one-tap buttons go away.

## Write a reply

**On iPhone:** type in the `Reply…` box and tap `Send`. Tap the expand icon for a bigger box. With an empty box, `Draft a reply` suggests one based on the conversation.

**On the web:** type in the box and click send, or press ⌘+Enter (Ctrl+Enter on Windows).

Replies can be up to 5,000 characters.

## Add a photo

On iPhone, tap the photo button next to the reply box, then `Take photo` or `Choose from library`. The photo stays in that topic, on the record. Photos can be viewed on the web, but they're added from the iPhone app.
