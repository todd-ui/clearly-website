## On iPhone

1. On **Topics**, tap **+**, or tap `New topic` on Today.
2. Write what you'd like to talk about.
3. Tap `Preview`. Clearly shows how it will be sent: as a quick question, a question, an FYI or a conversation.
4. Tap `Post`.

## On the web

1. On **Topics**, click `New topic`.
2. Say what you need.
3. Click `Post topic`.

## If there's already a topic like it

Clearly tells you when an open topic looks the same. You can add your message there as a reply, or post it as a new topic anyway.

## Start a topic about something specific

From an event, an expense or a day on the calendar, use `Ask about this`. Clearly starts a topic that refers to it.

> Important: Posts can't be edited or deleted once they're posted. Read it through before you post.
