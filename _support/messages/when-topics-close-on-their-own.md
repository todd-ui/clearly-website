So open topics don't pile up, Clearly closes them on its own.

- **No activity for 30 days:** the topic closes as `Closed · no activity` or `Closed · no reply`. You get a heads-up 2 days before.
- **A proposal not accepted within 30 days:** the topic closes as `Closed · not accepted`. The parent who hasn't answered gets a heads-up 3 days before.

Any new message restarts the 30 days. In the last 7 days, the topic shows `Closes in N days`.

Closed topics stay on the record, in full. To pick it up again, start a new topic.
