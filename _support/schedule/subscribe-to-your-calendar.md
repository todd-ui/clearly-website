Your calendar link shows the custody schedule, holidays and summer, and accepted swaps in your own calendar app. It's read-only: changes are made in Clearly.

## On the web

1. Go to **Shared**, then `Calendar sharing`.
2. Click `Turn on my calendar link`.
3. Click `Add to Apple or Outlook` or `Add to Google Calendar`, or `Copy` the link.

## On iPhone

1. Open **Settings**, then **Calendar sharing**.
2. Turn on `Calendar feed`.
3. Tap `Add to calendar`, `Copy link` or `Share link`.

## Keep it private

Anyone with your link can see the schedule. If you shared it by mistake, make a new link (`Make a new link` on the web, `Generate new link` on iPhone). The old one stops working. Your co-parent's link isn't affected.

To stop it altogether, click `Turn off` on the web, or turn off `Calendar feed` on iPhone.

Calendar apps refresh on their own schedule. Google Calendar can take up to a day. See [The calendar link isn't updating](/support/calendar-feed-not-updating).
