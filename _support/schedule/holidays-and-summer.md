## Holidays

**On the web:** go to **Shared**, then `Custody schedule`, then `Edit holidays`. **On iPhone:** go to **Shared**, then **Plan**, then `Holidays`.

- Switch between years with the arrows.
- Use the swap arrows on a holiday to change who has it that year. Holidays alternate each year.
- Turn on `Include the weekend` to add the weekend next to it.
- Add a holiday from the list, or add your own, such as a school break or a birthday, with its dates and who has it. Mark it as every year if the dates repeat.
- Stop tracking a holiday you don't alternate.

The **Holidays** tab in the iPhone **Schedule** shows who has each holiday by year.

## Summer

**On the web:** `Edit summer`. **On iPhone:** **Shared**, then **Plan**, then `Summer schedule`.

1. Say whether summer is different from the rest of the year.
2. Set when it starts and ends. Dates are kept as, for example, "the second Saturday in June", so they work every year.
3. Choose a pattern: alternating weeks, alternating two weeks, alternating months, or extended time with one parent.

Summer repeats every year until you change it.

> Note: A summer drawn day by day on iPhone is replaced if you save a pattern on the web. Clearly warns you before it does.
