## Patterns

- Week on / week off
- 2-2-3
- 5-2-2-5
- 3-4-4-3
- Every other weekend
- Custom: draw your own, up to 4 weeks long

## On the web

1. In the sidebar, go to **Shared**, then `Custody schedule`, and click `Set up` or `Change`.
2. Choose a pattern.
3. Say who has Week 1 (or who has the kids on the start date) and pick the start date.
4. Set the exchange time.
5. Check the four-week preview. Click a day to make its week Week 1. Then save.

## On iPhone

1. Go to the **Shared** tab, then **Plan**, and tap `Custody schedule`.
2. Choose a pattern and who starts.
3. In the preview, tap a week to shift the schedule, or tap a day to choose which week is Week 1.
4. Set the exchange time and tap `Save`.

## Good to know

- Either parent can change the schedule. The change shows for both of you straight away.
- Holidays and summer sit on top of the regular pattern. See [Holidays and summer](/support/holidays-and-summer).
- For a one-off change, use a swap request instead. See [Request or answer a swap](/support/request-or-answer-a-swap).
