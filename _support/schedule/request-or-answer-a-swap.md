## Ask for a swap

**On iPhone:**

1. On **Schedule**, open the `Swaps` tab and tap `Request swap`, or use `Swap request` on Today.
2. Choose `Request days` (days you'd like) or `Offer days` (days you'll give).
3. Pick the dates. They have to be today or later.
4. Add a note if you like, and send it.

**On the web:**

1. On **Schedule**, click `Request swap`.
2. Choose the day you'd like and, if you like, a day you'll give in return.
3. Add a note and click `Send request`.

Your co-parent is told.

## Answer a swap

Open the request and choose `Accept` or `Decline`. The other parent is told. An accepted swap changes the calendar for both of you.

## Withdraw or undo

- To withdraw a request before it's answered, open it and choose `Withdraw` on the web, or **•••**, then `Cancel request` on iPhone.
- On iPhone, the parent who asked can remove an accepted swap until one of its days has passed. The original schedule comes back.

## Expiry

A request that isn't answered expires after 7 days. On iPhone, cards show `Expires in N days`.
