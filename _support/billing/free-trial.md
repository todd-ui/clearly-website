- The trial is 14 days, for people who haven't had a Clearly subscription before.
- **On the web,** a card is needed to start it. **On iPhone,** it's Apple's free trial, through your Apple ID.
- If you cancel before the trial ends, you aren't charged. On iPhone, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
- If you don't cancel, your plan starts when the trial ends.

## If you've had a subscription before

The trial is for new subscribers only. On the web, the button tells you that you'll be charged today.

## After a free month from a professional

On the web, a free month from a professional takes the place of the trial. See [A free month from a professional](/support/free-month-from-a-professional).

## When the trial ends without a subscription

Clearly becomes read-only for your family. Everything you've recorded is kept, and you can still read, search and export it.
