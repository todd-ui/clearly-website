1. Sign in at [web.getclearly.app](https://web.getclearly.app).
2. Go to **Settings**, then **Subscription**, or click `Start your free trial` in the sidebar.
3. Choose `Annual` or `Monthly`.
4. Check the email you're subscribing as. If it's not you, sign out first.
5. Click the button to start your trial or subscribe, and enter your card details.

Payment is handled by our payment provider. Clearly never sees your card number.

When it's done, you see `Payment received. Updating your account…`. It can take a moment. You won't be charged twice.

## From the iPhone app

In the US, the iPhone plans screen has a link to subscribe on the web. It opens Safari already signed in, and brings you back to the app after you've paid. See [Subscribe in the iPhone app](/support/subscribe-in-the-iphone-app).
