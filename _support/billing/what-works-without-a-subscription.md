## Always free

- Reading everything in your record
- Searching it
- Exporting PDFs and spreadsheets
- Setting up your family before your co-parent joins
- Sharing with a professional
- Your calendar link

## Needs a subscription

- Inviting your co-parent
- Starting topics and replying
- Logging and changing expenses, events and swap requests
- Changing the shared plan once your co-parent has joined
- Importing and adding records

One subscription covers both parents.

## When a subscription ends

Clearly becomes read-only for the family. Nothing is deleted. Everything you've recorded stays yours to read, search and export, whatever happens.
