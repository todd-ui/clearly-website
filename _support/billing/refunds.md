## App Store purchases

Apple handles refunds under its own policies. Ask at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Web purchases

We don't refund time you don't use. If we charged you by mistake, for example twice, or after you cancelled, email [hello@getclearly.app](mailto:hello@getclearly.app) within 30 days and we'll refund it. This doesn't limit any refund the law where you live gives you.

A refunded charge ends that subscription straight away. If the charge was shared through Expenses and is still unpaid, that expense is cancelled.

See the [terms](https://getclearly.app/terms) for the full policy.
