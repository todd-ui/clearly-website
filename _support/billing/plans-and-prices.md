- **Monthly:** $24.99 a month. Cancel anytime.
- **Annual:** $249 a year.

These are US prices. In the iPhone app, the App Store shows the price for your country. On the web, sales tax may be added at checkout where it applies.

## One subscription for the family

Whichever parent subscribes covers both of you, on the web and iPhone. Your co-parent never pays to join. See [One subscription covers both parents](/support/one-subscription-covers-both-parents).

## Free

- Reading, searching and exporting your record, always.
- The demo in the iPhone app.
- Professionals, always.

See [What works without a subscription](/support/what-works-without-a-subscription).
