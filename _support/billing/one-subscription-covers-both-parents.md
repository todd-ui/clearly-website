A Clearly subscription belongs to the family. Whichever of you pays, it covers you both, wherever you use Clearly. It doesn't matter whether it was bought on the web or in the iPhone app.

## What the other parent sees

- **On the web:** `[Name]'s subscription covers your family.` and no plans to buy.
- **On iPhone:** `Your family is covered`, and **Settings** shows `Paid by your co-parent`.

A co-parent who joins after the subscription started is covered as soon as they join.

## No double charges

If your family is already covered, Clearly won't let either of you buy a second subscription on the web. If it happens anyway, see [If you both paid](/support/if-you-both-paid).

## Sharing the cost

The parent who pays can add each charge to Expenses so you split it. See [Share the cost of Clearly through Expenses](/support/share-the-subscription-cost).

## If the paying parent leaves Clearly

The family keeps access to the end of the period that was paid for. After that, either of you can subscribe.
