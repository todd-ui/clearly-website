## From a Clearly invitation

If a professional invited you to Clearly with a free month:

- It's 30 days of full access, on the web and iPhone. No card is needed.
- It starts when your family is set up, whichever app you use, as long as you sign up with the email they invited.
- It's yours whether or not you share anything with them.
- It covers your co-parent too.
- Each parent can have one free month. It isn't added if your family already pays.

Clearly tells you when it starts and when it ends. **Settings** shows the end date. After that, subscribe to keep posting. Reading stays free.

## From an App Store code

Some professionals hand out App Store codes for a 30-day trial. Redeem it on your iPhone:

1. Open the App Store app.
2. Tap your profile picture at the top right.
3. Tap **Redeem Gift Card or Code** and enter the code.

It covers both parents. After 30 days it renews at the monthly price unless you cancel.

Clearly never pays professionals for inviting families.
