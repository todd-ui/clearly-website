## On the web

1. Press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl+K on Windows, or click `Search` in the sidebar.
2. Type at least 2 characters. Try a word like "dentist" or "summer camp", a name, or an amount like "45".
3. Use the arrow keys and press Enter to open a result, or click it.

Results are grouped into Topics (including the text of messages), Schedule, Expenses and Shared.

## On iPhone

On **Topics**, use the search box at the top. You can search for words, or ask a question such as what was resolved about pickups, and tap `Ask Clearly`.
