## The sidebar

- **Today:** who has the kids, the next exchange, what needs you, and what's coming up.
- **Topics:** your conversations.
- **Schedule:** the calendar, events and swap requests.
- **Expenses:** what's owed and what's settled.
- **Shared:** `Kids`, `Contacts`, `Custody schedule`, `Shared expenses` (the split) and `Calendar sharing`.
- **Record:** `Records` (exports) and `Professionals`.
- **Settings**, at the bottom, with your name and whether your co-parent has joined.

Numbers next to Today, Topics and Expenses show what's waiting on you.

On a phone, tap the menu button at the top left to open the sidebar. A dot on it means something needs you.

## Today

- **Custody card:** who has the kids today, the next exchange, and the next 14 days.
- **For you:** topics, expenses and swap requests waiting on you.
- **Coming up:** the next five events.

## The setup card

Until setup is done, `Set up your family` on Today shows the steps left. See [Set up your family](/support/set-up-your-family).

## The tour

The tour runs once in each browser. To see it again, go to **Settings**, then **Appearance**, then `Take the tour`. If it doesn't start, reload the page.
