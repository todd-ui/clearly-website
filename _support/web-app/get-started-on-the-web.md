Clearly on the web is the full app in a browser. It's the same account and the same record as the iPhone app.

1. Go to [web.getclearly.app](https://web.getclearly.app).
2. Sign in with your email and password, or click `Create an account`.

It works in current versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox. Keep your browser up to date.

## On a shared computer

Click **Settings**, then **Account**, then `Sign out` when you're done. Your appearance choice and a few display preferences are remembered per browser.

## When Clearly updates

If you see `Clearly has been updated.`, click `Refresh` to get the new version. Nothing you saved is lost.
