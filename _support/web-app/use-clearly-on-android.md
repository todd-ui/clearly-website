Clearly doesn't have an app in Google Play. On Android, use Clearly on the web. It has everything your co-parent has on iPhone, on the same record.

1. Open Chrome and go to [web.getclearly.app](https://web.getclearly.app).
2. Sign in, or create your account. If your co-parent sent you an invite link, tap it first.

## Put it on your home screen

1. In Chrome, tap the menu (three dots).
2. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

Clearly then opens from its own icon.

## Staying up to date

Clearly on the web doesn't send phone notifications. Turn on emails instead, so you know when something needs you. See [Email notifications](/support/email-notifications).
