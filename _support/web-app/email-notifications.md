## Change your emails

**On the web:** go to **Settings**, then **Email notifications**.

**On iPhone:** open **Settings**, then **Email notifications**.

Turn `Email me` on or off, and choose `Topics and replies`, `Schedule` and `Expenses`. It's one setting, so a change on the web also applies on iPhone.

## What's in them

An email tells you what happened, for example that your co-parent replied in a topic, and links to it. It never includes what anyone wrote. You get at most one email per topic every 30 minutes.

## Emails you always get

Clearly always emails you when your co-parent joins, and when professional access to your record changes.
