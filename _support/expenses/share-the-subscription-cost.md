One parent pays for the family's subscription. If you're that parent, you can share the cost with your co-parent through Expenses.

## Turn it on

Right after you subscribe, Clearly asks whether to share the cost and suggests your usual expense split. Choose a split and confirm, or choose not to.

To change it later:

- **On the web:** **Settings**, then **Subscription**, then `Change` or `Stop sharing`.
- **On iPhone:** **Settings**, then **Subscription**, then `Sharing the cost`.

## How it works

- You're still the one charged. Billing doesn't change.
- Each paid charge after that is added to Expenses as an unpaid expense, logged by you, at the split you chose. Your co-parent settles it the usual way.
- Changes apply from the next charge. Expenses already logged keep their split.
- Trials and free months never create an expense.
- If a charge is refunded, its expense is cancelled if it's still unpaid.

Both parents can see whether the cost is shared. Only the parent who pays can change it.

> Note: This needs your co-parent to have joined, and works for US dollar subscriptions.
