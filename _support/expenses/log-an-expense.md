## On iPhone

1. On **Expenses**, tap `Add expense`, or use `New expense` on Today.
2. Enter the amount and what it was for.
3. Pick a category: Medical, Education, Sports, Clothing, Activities or Other.
4. Check the split. It comes from your plan. Tap `Change` to use a different split for this expense only.
5. Add a receipt photo if you have one.
6. Save it.

## On the web

1. On **Expenses**, click to log an expense.
2. Fill in `What it was for`, `Amount` and `Category`.
3. Adjust the split for this expense if you need to.
4. Add a receipt (a photo or PDF) if you have one.
5. Click `Log expense`.

Your co-parent is told, and sees their share under **Expenses**.

> Tip: Set how you'd like to be paid back first, so your co-parent can pay you in one tap. See [Set how you get paid back](/support/set-how-you-get-paid-back).
