Clearly doesn't move money. It opens the payment app your co-parent uses, with the amount filled in, and keeps track of what's settled.

## Pay your share

1. Open the expense under **Expenses**.
2. Tap or click `Pay $X with Venmo` (or PayPal, or Cash App). The app or site opens with the amount filled in.
3. When you come back, Clearly asks whether you sent it. Choose `Yes`, and it's marked paid.

If your co-parent prefers Zelle, cash or a check, the expense shows their details with a `Copy` button. Pay them, then mark it paid.

## Mark it paid

Use `Mark paid` (iPhone) or `Mark my $X paid` (web). Your co-parent is told.

If there's no way to pay shown, your co-parent hasn't said how they'd like to be paid back yet.

## Totals

**Expenses** shows what you're owed and what you owe. On the web you can filter by `Unpaid`, `Paid` and `Disputed`.
