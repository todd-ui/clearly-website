## For all new expenses

**On the web:** go to **Shared**, then `Shared expenses`. Pick a preset from 30/70 to 70/30, use the slider, or type your share exactly.

**On iPhone:** go to **Shared**, then **Plan**, then `Shared expenses`. Use the slider or a quick pick, or enter your share exactly.

The new split applies to expenses logged from then on. Expenses already logged keep their split.

## For one expense

When you log an expense, change the split for that one before you save it. On iPhone, tap `Change`. On the web, move the slider.
