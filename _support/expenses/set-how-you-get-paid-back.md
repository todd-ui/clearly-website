**On the web:** go to **Settings**, then **Getting paid back**. **On iPhone:** open **Settings**, then **Preferences**, then `Reimbursement`.

Choose Venmo, PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, Cash or Check, and enter your details:

- Venmo: your username
- PayPal: your PayPal.me username
- Cash App: your $Cashtag
- Zelle: the email or phone number you use for Zelle

On the web, a preview shows what your co-parent sees. Your co-parent sees these details only on expenses where they owe you.
