Only the parent who logged an expense can edit or delete it, and only while it's unpaid.

**On iPhone:** open the expense, tap **•••**, then `Edit expense` or `Delete expense`.

**On the web:** open the expense and use the edit or delete buttons.

Once an expense is marked paid, it can't be changed.

## If you don't agree with an expense

Use `Ask about this` on the expense to start a topic about it. The topic stays linked to it, on the record.
