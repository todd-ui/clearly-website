**On the web:** go to **Settings**, then **Profile**, change your first or last name and click `Save name`.

**On iPhone:** open **Settings**, tap your profile card, change your first or last name, and tap `Save`.

Your co-parent sees your first name day to day. Professionals and the PDF records show your full name.

To change the family's name instead, see [Your shared plan](/support/what-is-the-shared-plan).
