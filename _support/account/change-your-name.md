**On the web:** go to **Settings**, then **Profile**, change `Your name` and click `Save name`.

**On iPhone:** open **Settings**, tap your profile card, and change `Display name`.

To change the family's name instead, see [Your shared plan](/support/what-is-the-shared-plan).
