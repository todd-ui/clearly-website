- **Who sees your record:** you, your co-parent, and any professional you or your co-parent invite, for the areas shared. You're told whenever a professional is invited, and you can see what they view.
- **Protected:** your information is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- **Not sold:** Clearly doesn't sell your information, rent it or use it for advertising. There are no ads.
- **AI:** what you write in topics is sent to Anthropic to provide the AI features. Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. See [How Clearly uses AI](/support/how-clearly-uses-ai).
- **Payments:** card details go to our payment provider or Apple. Clearly never sees your card number.
- **Emails:** notification emails never include what anyone wrote.
- **Passwords:** Clearly turns down passwords found in known data breaches.
- **Your calendar link:** anyone with it can see the schedule, so keep it private, or make a new one.

Read the full [privacy policy](https://getclearly.app/privacy).
