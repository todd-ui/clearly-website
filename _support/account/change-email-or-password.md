## On the web

Go to **Settings**, then **Signing in**.

- **Email:** enter the new address. Clearly sends a confirmation link to both the old and the new address. The change takes effect when you confirm.
- **Password:** enter the new password twice and click `Change password`.

## On iPhone

Open **Settings** and tap your profile card.

- **Email:** change it and tap `Send link`, then confirm from your email.
- **Password:** tap `Change password` and `Send link`. Open the email on your iPhone and set the new password.

Passwords need at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number.
