Deleting your account can't be undone. You may want to [export your record](/support/export-your-record) first.

## On the web

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Account**, then `Delete my account`.
2. Read what will happen, type DELETE, and click `Delete for good`.

## On iPhone

1. Open **Settings** and tap your profile card.
2. Tap `Delete account` and confirm each step.

## What happens

- Your profile and login are deleted, along with your swap requests and your exports.
- **If your co-parent uses Clearly,** what you shared (topics, messages, expenses and events) stays in their record, shown as "Former co-parent".
- **If your co-parent hasn't joined,** the family's record is deleted, files included.
- Any professional's access you shared ends.
- You get a confirmation email.

## Your subscription

- **Web:** it stops renewing.
- **App Store:** deleting your account doesn't cancel it. Cancel it in the iPhone Settings app first. See [Cancel your subscription](/support/cancel-your-subscription).

Either way, your family keeps Clearly to the end of the period that was paid for.
