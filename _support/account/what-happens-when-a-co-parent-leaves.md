If your co-parent deletes their account:

- You keep the record. Their topics, messages, expenses, events and receipt photos stay, shown as "Former co-parent".
- Accepted swaps stay. Requests they hadn't answered are removed.
- A resolution they had proposed and you hadn't answered is set aside, so the topic is open again.
- The plan and expense split stay, from your side.
- If they paid for the subscription, your family keeps access to the end of the period they paid for. After that, you can subscribe.

You can invite a new co-parent later. A family has two parents at most.
