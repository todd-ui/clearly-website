Clearly keeps parent and professional accounts apart, so a professional never gets a parent's access by mistake.

- Parents sign in at [web.getclearly.app/login](https://web.getclearly.app/login).
- Professionals sign in at [web.getclearly.app/pro/login](https://web.getclearly.app/pro/login).

If you're a professional who is also a co-parent in Clearly, use a different email for each.

If you see "You're signed in with a parent account", sign out and sign in with your professional account.
