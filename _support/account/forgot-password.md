## On the web

1. Go to [web.getclearly.app/login](https://web.getclearly.app/login) (or [/pro/login](https://web.getclearly.app/pro/login) for professionals).
2. Enter your email, then click `Forgot your password?`.
3. Open the email from Clearly and click the link.
4. On `Choose a new password`, enter it and click `Save password`.

## On iPhone

1. On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap `Forgot password?`.
2. Open the email on your iPhone and tap the link. Clearly opens on `Reset password`.
3. Enter the new password twice and tap `Update password`.

It's the same password for the web and iPhone.

If the email doesn't arrive, check spam or junk, and wait a minute before asking again. See [I can't sign in](/support/cant-sign-in).
