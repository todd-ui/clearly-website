---
title: I'm not getting notifications on my iPhone
summary: Check that notifications are allowed for Clearly in iOS Settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't hiding them.
apps: [iphone]
keywords: [no notifications, push not working, alerts missing, badge]
order: 2
related: [iphone-notifications, not-getting-emails]
---
1. Open the iPhone **Settings** app, tap **Clearly**, then **Notifications**, and turn on **Allow Notifications**. Check that Lock Screen, Notification Center and Banners are on.
2. Check that a Focus mode or Do Not Disturb isn't on, or allow Clearly in it.
3. Make sure you're signed in to Clearly on this iPhone. Notifications go to the phone you're signed in on.
4. Update Clearly to the latest version from the App Store.
5. Sign out of Clearly and sign in again.

Clearly on the web doesn't send phone notifications. Use emails there. See [Email notifications](/support/email-notifications).
