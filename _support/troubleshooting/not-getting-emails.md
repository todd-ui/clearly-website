title: I'm not getting emails from Clearly
summary: Check your spam folder, your email settings in Clearly, and add hello@getclearly.app to your contacts.
apps: [iphone, web, pro]

1. **Look in spam or junk,** and in tabs like Promotions or Updates.
2. **Add hello@getclearly.app to your contacts,** so future emails arrive in your inbox.
3. **Check your settings.** On the web: **Settings**, then **Email notifications**. On iPhone: **Settings**, then **Email notifications**. Make sure `Email me` is on.
4. **Check the address.** On the web, **Settings**, then **Profile**, shows the email on your account.
5. **Wait a minute** before asking for another reset or invite email. Too many in a row are paused.

Notification emails are sent at most once per topic every 30 minutes, so several replies arrive as one email.
