1. **Wait a moment.** Right after paying on the web, Clearly shows `Payment received. Updating your account…`. It can take a minute. You won't be charged twice.
2. **Check the account.** The subscription belongs to the family of the account that bought it. Check that you're signed in with the same email.
3. **On iPhone, restore it.** Open the plans screen and tap `Restore purchases`.
4. **Refresh.** Reload the page on the web, or close and reopen the iPhone app.
5. **Check your co-parent has joined.** A subscription covers your co-parent once they're in your family.

If it still isn't showing, email [hello@getclearly.app](mailto:hello@getclearly.app) with the email on your account and roughly when you paid. Please don't send card details.
