## On iPhone

1. Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data. Clearly shows a banner when you're offline.
2. If you see `Having trouble connecting`, tap `Retry`.
3. Close Clearly fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again.
4. Update Clearly from the App Store.

## On the web

1. Reload the page.
2. If you see `Something went wrong`, click `Reload`. Nothing you saved is lost.
3. If you see `Clearly has been updated`, click `Refresh`.
4. Try another current browser, or turn off extensions that block scripts.

Your record is stored safely on Clearly's servers, not on your device, so a problem with the app doesn't lose anything.

If it keeps happening, email [hello@getclearly.app](mailto:hello@getclearly.app) with what you see, your device, and the browser or app version.
