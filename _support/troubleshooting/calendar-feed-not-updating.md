Your calendar app, not Clearly, decides how often to check the link.

- **Google Calendar** can take up to a day.
- **Apple Calendar:** open the iPhone **Settings** app, tap **Calendar**, then **Accounts**, then **Fetch New Data**, and choose a shorter interval.
- **Outlook** refreshes every few hours.

If nothing shows at all, check the link is still on in Clearly. If you made a new link, the old one stopped working. Add the new one.
