- **"Code wasn't found" or "didn't match":** check each character. Codes are 6 characters and never contain the letters I or O or the numbers 0 or 1. Ask your co-parent to copy it again from Clearly.
- **"Already has two parents":** a family has two parents at most. If you meant to start your own, choose `No, I'm starting` on the web, or `Start fresh` on iPhone.
- **"Too many incorrect codes":** wait an hour and try again.
- **The link opened, but nothing happened:** create your account in the same browser you opened the link in. In a private window, type the code instead.
- **An emailed invite expired:** it lasts 30 days. Ask your co-parent to resend it. The family code itself keeps working.
