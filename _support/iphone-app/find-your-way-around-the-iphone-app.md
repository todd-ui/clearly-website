## The tabs

- **Today:** who has the kids, the next exchange, what needs you, and what's coming up.
- **Topics:** your conversations. See [How topics work](/support/how-topics-work).
- **Schedule:** the calendar, swap requests and holidays.
- **Expenses:** what's unpaid and what's paid.
- **Shared:** your plan, kids and contacts.

## Badges

A red number on a tab means something is waiting on you: topics to read or answer, swap requests to answer, or expenses you owe. The app icon shows the total.

## Settings

Tap your initial in the circle at the top right of any tab. See [iPhone settings](/support/iphone-settings).

## Quick actions

On **Today**, the buttons for a swap request, a new expense, a new event and a new topic save a few taps. Choose which ones show in **Settings**, then **Quick actions**.

## Take the tour again

Open **Settings** and tap `Take the tour`.
