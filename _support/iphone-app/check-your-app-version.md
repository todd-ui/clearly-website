## Find your version

Open **Settings** in Clearly and scroll to the bottom. You'll see a line like "Clearly v2.0.7 (35)".

## Update

1. Open the App Store.
2. Tap your profile picture at the top right.
3. Find Clearly under updates and tap **Update**.

To update automatically, open the iPhone **Settings** app, tap **App Store** and turn on **App Updates**.

## "Update Clearly" screen

If Clearly shows `Update Clearly`, that version is no longer supported. Tap `Update` to open the App Store. Nothing you've recorded is lost.
