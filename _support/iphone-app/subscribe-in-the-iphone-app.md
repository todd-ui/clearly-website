## Subscribe through Apple

1. Open the plans screen. It appears when you invite your co-parent or try to post, and from the subscription card on **Today**.
2. Choose `Annual` or `Monthly`.
3. Tap `Start free trial`, or `Start annual` or `Start monthly` if you've had a trial before.
4. Confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your Apple ID password.

The prices shown come from the App Store in your country.

## Subscribe on the web instead (US)

In the US App Store, the plans screen also shows `Or subscribe on the web, at web.getclearly.app`. Tapping it opens Safari already signed in to your account. Once you've paid, Clearly sends you back to the app, where your subscription is active. This needs version 2.0.7 or later.

## If your co-parent already pays

The plans screen shows `Your family is covered`. There's nothing to buy.

## Restore a purchase

If you reinstalled the app or changed phones, open the plans screen and tap `Restore purchases`.

## Switch from monthly to annual

Open **Settings**, tap your subscription, then `Manage subscription` and `Upgrade to annual`.

> Note: The app doesn't take offer codes. If a professional gave you an App Store code, redeem it in the App Store app. See [A free month from a professional](/support/free-month-from-a-professional).
