Tap your initial at the top right of any tab to open **Settings**.

- **Profile card:** tap it to edit your display name and email, change your password, sign out or delete your account.
- **Subscription:** your plan, who pays, and `Manage subscription`. Also whether the cost is shared through Expenses.
- **Family:** the family name, which professionals you invite also see.
- **Co-parent:** your invite code, invite by email, or join with their code.
- **Professionals:** invite a professional and see who has access.
- **Calendar sharing:** your calendar link. See [Subscribe to your calendar](/support/subscribe-to-your-calendar).
- **Preferences:**
  - `Reimbursement`: how you get paid back for expenses.
  - `Email notifications`
  - `Reframing style`: the default style when Clearly suggests wording (Brief, Balanced or Warm).
  - `Color theme`: only on this phone.
  - `Quick actions` on Today.
  - `Take the tour`
- **Support:** `Send feedback`, `Export data`, `Help`, and the privacy policy and terms.

The version number is at the bottom, for example "Clearly v2.0.7 (35)".
