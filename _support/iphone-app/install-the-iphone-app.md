1. On your iPhone, open the [Clearly page in the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clearly-co-parenting-resolved/id6758027374).
2. Tap **Get**.
3. Open Clearly. Tap `Sign in` if you already have an account, or `Get started` to create one.

If you already use Clearly on the web, sign in with the same email and password. Your record, schedule and subscription are all there.

> Note: The app is for iPhone. On an Android phone, an iPad or a computer, use [Clearly on the web](/support/get-started-on-the-web), which has the same record.
