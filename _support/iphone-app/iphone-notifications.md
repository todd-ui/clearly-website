Clearly asks to send notifications once, after setup. You can change your answer at any time.

## Turn phone notifications on or off

1. Open the iPhone **Settings** app.
2. Scroll down and tap **Clearly**.
3. Tap **Notifications** and turn **Allow Notifications** on or off.

In Clearly you can also go to **Settings**, then **Email notifications**, then `Phone notifications`, which opens the same place.

## What you're notified about

- **Topics:** a new topic, a question, an FYI, a reply, an answer, a proposed resolution, and when a topic is resolved or goes back to talking.
- **Schedule:** a swap request, an accepted or declined swap, a removed swap, and a new event.
- **Expenses:** a new expense, and an expense marked paid.

Tap a notification to open the thing it's about.

## Emails

Emails are set separately. See [Email notifications](/support/email-notifications).
