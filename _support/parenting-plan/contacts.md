**On the web:** go to **Shared**, then `Contacts`, and click `Add contact`. **On iPhone:** go to **Shared**, then `Contacts`, and tap `Add contact`.

Choose a type (Doctor, Dentist, Hospital, School, Childcare, Activity, Therapist, Pharmacy, Family, Emergency or Other) and add the name, phone, email and address.

On iPhone, a contact has buttons to call, email or get directions.

Deleting a contact is permanent. To bring in contacts from your phone or another app, see [Bring your calendar, contacts and records](/support/bring-records-from-another-app).
