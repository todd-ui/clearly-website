**On the web:** click `Contacts` in the sidebar, then `Add a contact`. **On iPhone:** go to **Shared**, then `Contacts`, and tap `Add contact`.

Choose a type (Doctor, Dentist, Hospital, School, Childcare, Activity, Therapist, Pharmacy, Family, Emergency or Other) and add the name, phone, email and address.

## Finding a contact

On the web, contacts are grouped by type (Doctors, School, Family and so on), A to Z in each group. Names and addresses are tidied as you save them, so "201 hicks street" shows as "201 Hicks Street".

## Call, email or get directions

Each contact has buttons for what it has:

- **Directions** opens the address in Google Maps
- **Call** starts a call on your phone
- **Email** opens a new email

The iPhone app has the same buttons.

Deleting a contact is permanent. To bring in contacts from your phone or another app, see [Bring your calendar, contacts and records](/support/bring-records-from-another-app).
