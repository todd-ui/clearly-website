**On the web:** go to **Shared**, then `Kids`, and click `Add a child` or `Edit`. **On iPhone:** go to **Shared**, then `Kids`, and tap `Add child`.

For each child you can add:

- Name and birthday
- Allergies and preferences
- Medications, with the dose and how often
- Notes

On iPhone you can also pick a color for each child.

Removing a child can't be undone, so Clearly asks you to confirm. Two children can't have the same name.
