**On the web:** click `Kids` in the sidebar, then `Add a child`, or click a child to edit them. **On iPhone:** go to **Shared**, then `Kids`, and tap `Add child`.

For each child you can add:

- Name, birthday and a color
- Allergies and preferences
- Medications, with the dose and how often
- Notes

## Medication details

On the web, each medication also has:

- **What it's for**, such as asthma or a peanut reaction
- **Expires**, the expiration date, for an EpiPen or a prescription that needs renewing
- **Other info**, such as "with food", where it's kept or the prescribing doctor

The Kids page shows what each medication is for and when it expires. A medication that has expired, or expires within 30 days, is highlighted.

## Expiration dates on the shared calendar

When a medication has an expiration date, Clearly adds an all-day event to the shared calendar on that day, such as "Ash's EpiPen Jr expires", so both of you see it coming. Change the date and the event moves; remove the date or the medication and the event goes. The event also shows in [your calendar app](/support/subscribe-to-your-calendar) if you've connected it.

> Tip: Change the expiration date on the Kids page, not on the calendar. The calendar event follows the Kids page.

Removing a child can't be undone, so Clearly asks you to confirm. Two children can't have the same name.
