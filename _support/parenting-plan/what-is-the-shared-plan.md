**Shared** holds the parts of your parenting arrangement you both work from:

- The custody schedule, holidays and summer
- The expense split
- Your kids and their health details
- Contacts such as doctors, schools and coaches
- The family name

Either parent can change any of it, and the change shows for both of you straight away. Until your co-parent joins, changes are free. After that, changes need the family's subscription.

**On the web,** it's the **Shared** section of the sidebar. **On iPhone,** it's the **Shared** tab, with `Plan`, `Kids` and `Contacts`.

## The family name

The family name shows at the top of Clearly and to any professional you share with. **On the web,** click `Rename` on a Shared page. **On iPhone,** open **Settings**, then **Family**, then `Family name`. Leave it empty and Clearly uses both parents' names.
