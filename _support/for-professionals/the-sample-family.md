Every new professional account includes the Taylors, a fictional family, marked `Sample`. You're shown as Alex Taylor's family lawyer, with every area shared.

- A short tour shows what changed, messages exactly as written, and building an exhibit.
- Exhibits and PDFs work, and are marked as a sample.
- Nothing you do in the sample is saved or seen by anyone, and nothing is logged.

To remove it, choose `Hide sample case`. Bring it back from **Settings**, then **Getting started**.
