Exports need the family to share the **Records** area with you.

1. Open the case and go to **Records**.
2. Choose `Last 30 days`, `Last 90 days` or `All time`.
3. Choose children's full names or initials only, and a state for the declaration if needed.
4. Download:
   - **Full record (PDF)**, with a document ID on every page.
   - **An exhibit:** set dates and contents, click `Build timeline`, then `Exhibit PDF` or `CSV`.
   - **Spreadsheets:** topics, expenses, and schedule and swaps.
   - **Documents** the family imported from other apps.

Inside a topic, `Export this topic (PDF)` saves just that topic.

Each export is listed in the family's access log. Anyone can check a PDF at [getclearly.app/verify](https://getclearly.app/verify/).
