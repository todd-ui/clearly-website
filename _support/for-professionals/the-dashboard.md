Your **Dashboard** shows every case, and the ones with something new.

## Needs a look

Each case lists short updates, most urgent first:

- `Expense disputed`
- `Resolution waiting`: a proposed resolution hasn't been answered in 3 or more days.
- `Waiting for a reply`: a topic's last message is 2 or more days old.
- `Schedule change`: a swap is waiting, or was declined in the last 14 days.
- `Unpaid expense`: unpaid for 30 or more days.
- `New posts`: since your last visit.
- `Access ending`: within 14 days.

These are dated facts with a link to the item. Clearly never scores a parent or draws conclusions.

## Go through them

Click `Go through them` to open each case in turn. Each update ticks off when you open it. Click `Next` to move on, `Finish` at the end, or `Stop` at any time. Items you've seen stay hidden until they change.

## Your cases

The table lists each case with its parents, last activity and access. Use the `Active`, `Invited` and `Past` tabs in the sidebar.

In a case, press J or K (or ] and [) to move to the next or previous case.
