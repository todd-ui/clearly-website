Open **Settings** in the sidebar.

- **Profile:** your name, role, practice, phone, bar or license number, and an `Email for families` if you'd like families to reach you somewhere other than your sign-in email. Families who share with you see your name, role, practice, email and phone.
- **Families you invited:** resend, edit, cancel or delete invitations. See [Invite a family](/support/invite-a-family).
- **Notifications:**
  - Email me when a family changes what you can see or ends your access. Invitations are always emailed.
  - Choose which updates show on your Dashboard. Cases still show everything when you open them.
- **Appearance:** light, dark or automatic.
- **Getting started:** show the welcome steps again, take the tour, or bring back the sample family.
- **Inviting clients:** how a client can invite you from their own app.
- **Account:** `Sign out`.

## Close your professional account

Email [hello@getclearly.app](mailto:hello@getclearly.app) from your sign-in address and we'll close it.
