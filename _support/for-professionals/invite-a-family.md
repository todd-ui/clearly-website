1. On your **Dashboard**, click `Invite a family`.
2. Enter the parent's email and, if you like, their name.
3. Under `What you'd like them to share`, check the areas. Clearly suggests areas for your role. The parent can change them.
4. Leave `Include a free month of Clearly for them` on, or turn it off.
5. Add a short note if you like, such as "Ahead of our first session on Nov 12".
6. Click `Send invitation`.

The email comes from hello@getclearly.app, and replies go to you. If it doesn't arrive, ask them to check their spam folder.

## What happens next

- The parent sets up their family first. Then Clearly asks whether to share with you, and they choose the areas.
- Nothing is shared without their yes. If they share, their co-parent is told.
- The invitation shows as `Shared with you` once they do, and the case appears under `Active`.

## The free month

30 days of full access on the web and iPhone, no card needed, starting when they set up. It's a gift to the family, theirs whether or not they share. Clearly never pays professionals for referrals, and families are told so.

## Manage invitations

Go to **Settings**, then **Families you invited**, to `Resend`, `Edit`, cancel or delete one. You can send up to 25 invitations a day. An invitation expires 60 days after it was last sent.
