Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows), or click `Search` in the sidebar. Type at least 2 characters.

- **Cases** match by your case name, matter number, family name or either parent's name.
- **Topics** match words in topics and messages, in active cases that share Topics.

Tick `Include past cases` to search the copies kept from cases that have ended.

> Note: When a search returns a family's messages, it's listed in that family's access log, with the words you searched. Both parents can see it.
