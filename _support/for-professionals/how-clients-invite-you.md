Ask your client to:

1. Open Clearly on the web or iPhone.
2. Go to **Professionals** (in the web sidebar, or **Settings**, then **Professionals**, on iPhone).
3. Tap `Invite a professional`, enter your email and role, choose the areas to share, and send it.

You'll get an email with `View the invitation`. See [Accept a parent's invitation](/support/accept-a-parents-invitation).

Your client's co-parent is told when you're invited, and both parents see who has access.
