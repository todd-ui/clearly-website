When a parent invites you, you get an email saying they invited you to view their Clearly record.

1. Click `View the invitation`.
2. The page shows who invited you, in what role, the areas you'll see and any end date or note.
3. Click `Create free account` or `I have an account`, then sign in.
4. Click `Accept invitation`, or `Decline`. The parent is told either way.

You can also accept from the portal: invited cases are under the `Invited` tab in the sidebar.

## If it doesn't work

- **"Sent to a different email address":** sign in with the address the parent invited.
- **"No longer open":** invitations expire after 30 days. Ask the parent to send a new one.
- **"Signed in with a parent account":** sign out, and use a professional account.

Both parents can see that you have access and what you view.
