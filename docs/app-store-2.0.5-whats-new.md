# Clearly 2.0.5: App Store Connect text

## What's New in This Version

Smoother start, and Clearly on the web and iPhone now work as one.

- New to Clearly? You can now look around the app before starting your free trial. Your record is always free to read.
- Set up Clearly on the web? Sign in on your iPhone and go straight in, with your schedule, holidays and summer just as you left them.
- Stronger password protection when you reset your password.
- Cash App payment links now work however you typed your $Cashtag.
- Clearer wording in Professionals.
- Records and exhibits you export are now court-ready, with a document ID on every page that anyone can check at getclearly.app/verify.

## Notes for App Review (if asked)

- The paywall at the end of setup now has "Not now, go to Clearly". A parent who doesn't start the trial can use the app read-only; adding or changing anything shows the plans again. No change to the in-app purchases themselves.
- No new permissions, no new data collected.
