# Clearly 2.0.6: App Store Connect text

## What's New in This Version

Court-ready records, your way, and email that does what you choose.

- Build an exhibit: just the dates, parent, topic or words that matter, as a court-ready PDF with a document ID on every page.
- Show children as initials, and name your state in the declaration, on any PDF you export.
- Email notifications: turn them on or off, and pick topics, schedule or expenses. The same setting on your iPhone and on the web.
- See your lawyer's or mediator's email and phone in Professionals, and tap to get in touch.
- "Reframing style" (Brief, Balanced or Warm) sets how Clearly helps word a hard message.
- Clearer messages when there's nothing to export yet.

## Notes for App Review (if asked)

- No new permissions and no new data collected. Email notifications use the email address already on the account.
- Exhibits and records are made on Clearly's server, as before; the new screen only chooses what goes in them.
- No change to in-app purchases.
