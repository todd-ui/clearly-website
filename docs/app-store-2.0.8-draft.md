# Clearly 2.0.8: App Store Connect text (draft, not published)

Paste into App Store Connect when the 2.0.8 build (38) is submitted. Nothing here is live until you do.

2.0.7 went out as build 36. 2.0.8 adds what came after it: the kid colours that match the web, and the medication details.

## What's New in This Version

Your kids' details, the same on iPhone and on the web.

- Each child's color now matches everywhere: pick it on iPhone or on the web and both of you see the same one.
- Medications: add what each one is for and when it expires. Expiration dates go on the shared calendar for both of you, and you can tap a medication to change it.

## Promotional text

No change from 2.0.7.

## Notes for App Review

- No new permissions and no new data collected from the device.
- A medication's expiration date is saved with the child's details and shown as an all-day event on the family's shared calendar. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
