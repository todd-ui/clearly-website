Paste into App Store Connect when the 2.1.0 build is submitted. Nothing here is live until you do.

2.1.0 changes what happens when setup ends: new families go into the app first instead of straight to the plans, as on the web. If 2.0.8 never went live, keep its two lines (kid colors, medications) in What's New; if it did, delete them.

## What's New in This Version

A gentler start.

- When setup is done, you go straight into Clearly to look around. Start your 14-day free trial when you're ready to add something or invite your co-parent.
- Each child's color now matches everywhere: pick it on iPhone or on the web and both of you see the same one.
- Medications: add what each one is for and when it expires. Expiration dates go on the shared calendar for both of you.

## Notes for App Review

- No new permissions and no new data collected from the device.
- After setup, a new family now lands in the app. The subscription screen opens when they first post, add something or invite their co-parent, and from the free trial card on the Today screen. Purchases and Restore purchases work as before.
