Clearly on Android.

There’s no Android app to install. Clearly runs in Chrome on any Android phone.

It has everything your co-parent has on iPhone, on the same record.

Get started

Start in Chrome.

Open Chrome and go to web.getclearly.app. Sign in, or create your account. If your co-parent sent you an invite link, tap that first.

Put it on your home screen. In Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then Add to Home screen or Install app. Clearly then opens from its own icon.

Turn on emails. Clearly on the web doesn’t send phone notifications, so switch on emails in Settings to know when something needs you.

The same Clearly as iPhone.

Topics that end in an answer, help with wording, the custody schedule and swaps, expenses, kids and contacts, records and exports, and sharing with a professional. Changes show up for your co-parent straight away, whichever phone they use.

It works the same on a computer or an iPad, which helps when you’re writing something long or building an exhibit.

One parent on Android, one on iPhone.

That’s fine. You share one family, one record and one subscription. Your co-parent can use the companion iPhone app or the web, and you see the same thing.

Questions about Android.

Is there a Clearly app in Google Play? No. On Android, use Clearly on the web at web.getclearly.app in Chrome. You can add it to your home screen so it opens from its own icon.

Can I get notifications on Android? Clearly on the web doesn’t send phone notifications. Turn on emails in Settings so you know when something needs you.

Will my co-parent on iPhone see what I post? Yes. You share the same family and record, and changes show up straight away on both.

Does it cost more on Android? No. It’s $24.99 a month or $249 a year for the whole family, with a 14-day free trial, wherever you use it.

What Clearly is.

Clearly is co-parenting communication for the schedule, the money and the conversations. Topics end in a clear answer or an agreement you both accept, and when a conversation needs it, Clearly helps with the wording. You choose what to send, and every post stays on the record as written.
