[01] The record

What a Clearly record shows.

Each item is labeled, so a reader never has to guess whether something was said by a parent, agreed by both, or recorded by the system.

Parent messages
The original words of each parent, in order, with date and time. This is the canonical record.
System summaries
Topic summaries written by Clearly are labeled as system-generated and kept separate from what parents wrote.
Agreements
A topic is recorded as agreed only when both parents accept the same written resolution.
Not accepted
A resolution proposed but not accepted within 30 days closes as “not accepted.” It is never treated as agreed.
Inactive topics
A topic with no activity for 30 days closes and stays on the record, marked as a system action.
Schedule and expenses
Swap requests and their answers, shared expenses with receipts and splits, and changes to the parenting plan.

[02] Exports and limits

What you receive, and what Clearly doesn't do.

PDF export

  • Last 30 days, last 90 days, or all time
  • Index of topics, then full message timelines
  • Message bodies included by default
  • Provenance information and disclaimers

CSV export

  • All data as a spreadsheet
  • For detailed or forensic review
  • Consistent from one export to the next

Clearly does not

  • Give legal advice
  • Take sides or decide for either parent
  • Determine fault or intent
  • Interpret messages for the reader
  • Replace professional judgment

[04] Questions

Common questions.

Can these records be used as evidence?

Clearly produces time-stamped records of communication, kept as written. Whether any document is admissible depends on the jurisdiction, the case and the court. Clearly provides the record; evidentiary decisions belong to the court and counsel.

Does Clearly analyze or score either parent?

No. Clearly records what was communicated and when. It does not score, rank or evaluate behavior. Its tone check only suggests calmer wording to the person writing, before they post, and they choose what to send.

What happens when one parent doesn't respond?

Silence is never treated as agreement. A proposed resolution that isn't accepted within 30 days closes as “not accepted,” and a topic with no activity for 30 days closes and stays on the record. Both are labeled as system actions.

Can parents edit or delete messages?

No. Topics and replies can't be edited or deleted once posted. Exports include provenance information and disclaimers.

Does the other parent have to pay?

No. One subscription covers both parents. The co-parent joins free with an invite code. Without a subscription, Clearly is read-only and records stay viewable and exportable.
