[07] Questions

Common questions.

Is it really free for professionals?

Yes. Professional accounts are free, with no limit on cases. Families pay for their own Clearly subscription, and one subscription covers both parents.

What can I see in a family's record?

Only the areas the inviting parent chooses: topics and replies, the schedule, expenses, family info, records to export, and how often Clearly paused a message to suggest clearer wording. Access is always read-only, and it can have an end date.

Does the other parent know I have access?

Yes. The other parent is told whenever a professional is invited, when what they can see changes, and when access ends. Both parents can see who has access and what each professional opened.

Why is an area of a case greyed out?

The parent who shared the case hasn't shared that area with you. Parents choose each area on its own: topics, the schedule, expenses, family info and records. To see it, ask that parent to turn it on in Clearly, under Professionals on the web or Settings › Professionals on iPhone, then Change access. You'll see it as soon as they save, and the other parent is told what changed.

What happens when my access ends?

You keep a read-only copy of what you could see up to that moment, for your case file, along with your private notes. Nothing added to the family's record afterwards reaches it.

Can I invite my clients to Clearly?

Yes. Invite a family from your Dashboard by email, and resend, change or withdraw the invitation from Settings. Nothing is shared with you until a parent chooses to.

Can these records be used as evidence?

Clearly produces time-stamped records of communication, kept as written, with a document ID and page number on every page that anyone can check at getclearly.app/verify. Whether any document is admissible depends on the jurisdiction, the case and the court. Clearly provides the record; evidentiary decisions belong to the court and counsel.

How can a court check that a record is genuine?

Every Clearly record and exhibit has a document ID on every page and a QR code on its cover. Anyone can scan the code or drop the PDF into getclearly.app/verify: Clearly confirms it produced that exact file and that nothing in it has changed since. The file is checked in the browser and never uploaded.

Does Clearly analyze or score either parent?

No. Clearly records what was communicated and when. When a message needs it, Clearly suggests clearer wording to the person writing, before they post, and they choose what to send.

Does Clearly pay referral fees?

No. Clearly never pays professionals for referrals, and families are told so.
